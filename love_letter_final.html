To My Favourite Person,
My Home, My Love

There were days when I would sing, “Enakku oru girlfriend venum da…”, without knowing who she would be, without knowing where life was hiding her.

And then, somehow, you entered.

We didn't know each other before. We didn't plan any of this. Love didn't come because either of us asked for it. But some people enter your life so quietly, and then one day you realise they have become a part of every tomorrow you imagine.

Maybe love doesn't arrive when we go looking for it. Maybe it arrives when two paths meet, when two strangers decide to walk a little further together. And perhaps, if they keep walking through every turn, every distance, every uncertain road, somewhere along the way, they find their destiny in each other.

For me, meeting you was one of those turning points. A small moment that slowly changed everything that came after it.

You were beautiful—not just in the way the world sees beauty. You were beautiful in the way you thought, in the way you felt things, and in the little sparkle that appeared in your eyes. There was something about you that made me want to know you more, listen to you more, and somehow keep you close to my heart.

And slowly, without me even noticing when it happened, you became someone my mind kept returning to.

I look at the moon, and I think about you. I listen to a song, and somewhere between the lyrics, I find you. I can be sitting with my family, surrounded by everyone, and still, for a moment, wonder what you're doing. I see two people in love, and somehow, I think of us.

A place, a song, a conversation, a random little thing—sometimes it takes almost nothing for the world to remind me of you. Because wherever I am, whatever I am doing, and no matter who I am with, some part of my heart is always thinking about you.

What more could I have done,
When you had already given me your heart?
What more could I have said,
When you understood me without a word?

I could search every road,
Cross every sky, build every dream—
But somehow, the greatest thing I ever did
Was simply find you. ❤️

As long as I exist, you will be loved. Always.

Always yours,
Yours, in every version of me.
